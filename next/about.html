Sources et attributions

PeakSurf assemble des données publiques et des modèles ouverts, puis les interprète avec une intelligence artificielle. Merci à leurs producteurs.

Océan : Copernicus Marine Service

Produit à partir d’informations du Copernicus Marine Service de l’Union européenne.
Generated using E.U. Copernicus Marine Service Information.

  • Vagues : Atlantique, Manche, mer du Nord — modèle IBI (Iberia-Biscay-Ireland) Ocean Wave Analysis and Forecast, produit IBI_ANALYSISFORECAST_WAV_005_005, jeu cmems_mod_ibi_wav_anfc_0.027deg — doi:10.48670/moi-00025. Réanalyse cmems_mod_ibi_wav_my_0.027deg pour la climatologie.
  • Vagues : Méditerranée — modèle MED, jeux cmems_mod_med_wav_anfc_4.2km (prévision) et cmems_mod_med_wav_my_4.2km (climatologie).
  • Vagues : hors de ces domaines — modèle global, jeux cmems_mod_glo_wav_anfc_0.083deg et cmems_mod_glo_wav_my_0.2deg, le cas échéant.
  • Niveau de la mer et température de l’eau — modèle IBI physique, jeux cmems_mod_ibi_phy_anfc_0.027deg-2D (variables zos, thetao).

Conditions : licence Copernicus Marine.

Observations et intelligence artificielle

Le modèle qui note chaque heure (Bon / Moyen / Mauvais) et estime la hauteur des vagues à la plage a été entraîné sur des observations participatives publiées par des surfeurs sur surf-report.com, croisées avec les données ci-dessus. Il estime ce qu’un surfeur aurait observé ; ce n’est pas une mesure.

Sources & attributions

PeakSurf combines public data and open models, then interprets them with machine learning. Thanks to the people who produce them.

Ocean: Copernicus Marine Service

Generated using E.U. Copernicus Marine Service Information.

  • Waves: Atlantic, Channel, North Sea — IBI (Iberia-Biscay-Ireland) Ocean Wave Analysis and Forecast, product IBI_ANALYSISFORECAST_WAV_005_005, dataset cmems_mod_ibi_wav_anfc_0.027deg — doi:10.48670/moi-00025. Reanalysis cmems_mod_ibi_wav_my_0.027deg for the climatology.
  • Waves: Mediterranean — MED model, datasets cmems_mod_med_wav_anfc_4.2km (forecast) and cmems_mod_med_wav_my_4.2km (climatology).
  • Waves: outside those domains — global model, datasets cmems_mod_glo_wav_anfc_0.083deg and cmems_mod_glo_wav_my_0.2deg, where used.
  • Sea level and water temperature — IBI physics model, dataset cmems_mod_ibi_phy_anfc_0.027deg-2D (variables zos, thetao).

Terms: Copernicus Marine licence.

Weather: Open-Meteo

Weather data (wind, gusts, day/night) by Open-Meteo.com, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Open-Meteo aggregates models from national weather services (Météo-France, ECMWF, DWD and others).

Wind climatology: Open-Meteo historical archive, based on the ERA5 reanalysis. Contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information.

Tides

  • Tide predictions © SHOM (French Navy Hydrographic and Oceanographic Service), via data.shom.fr. SHOM bathymetry is also used to estimate beach slopes.
  • As a fallback, sea level from the Copernicus IBI model (above) or the FES2014 tide model: FES2014 was produced by Noveltis, Legos and CLS and distributed by Aviso+, with support from CNES (aviso.altimetry.fr).

Maps

  • Base map © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); tiles © Esri (Esri, HERE, Garmin).
  • Land outlines (ocean map): Natural Earth (public domain).

Observations and machine learning

The model that rates each hour (Good / Fair / Poor) and estimates the wave height at the beach was trained on crowd-sourced reports posted by surfers on surf-report.com, matched with the data above. It estimates what a surfer would have observed; it is not a measurement.

Webcams

Embedded video streams belong to their operators (GoSurf, Viewsurf). Each player links to the camera’s source page.
