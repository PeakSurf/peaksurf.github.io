About this map

Colours are Copernicus Marine IBI total significant wave height at native 1/36° resolution; the scale is fixed across the full forecast. Point readings use native model values. Land is Natural Earth. Model source.

Rating Definitions
Good — Optimal conditions. Clean faces, offshore wind, and solid set waves.
Fair — Surfable but imperfect. Light texture, cross-shore wind, or inconsistent sets.
Poor — Blown out, too small, or closing out. Not recommended.
Wind Indicators
Offshore — Favorable: wind blowing from land out to sea, grooming wave faces.
Cross-shore — Neutral: wind along the coast. Texture depends on strength.
Onshore — Unfavorable: wind blowing from sea to land, chopping up the surface.
Numbers — Mean wind speed (km/h) with the gust shown below as G<value>. Compare the two to see how gusty the session is.
